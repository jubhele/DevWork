### Case Study 4: Public Perimeter — Informal Occupation Detection

**Scenario**: Section of south-east boundary borders public open land; area is vegetated and not on a regular guard patrol route.

**Without Drone**:
- Situation not detected until guard physically walks that section — may be days or weeks
- By the time discovery is made, informal structures may be established
- Removal becomes a more complex and potentially confrontational process
- No documented evidence of when occupation began

**With Drone**:
1. Scheduled aerial sweep identifies early signs: makeshift shelter, fire materials, individuals
2. Control Room notifies site management with aerial footage same day
3. AECI coordinates response (SAPS, legal, internal security) while situation is still manageable
4. Drone documents before, during, and after the response — clean evidence trail if legal action follows
5. Subsequent sweeps confirm area is clear; ongoing monitoring continues

**Value**: Early detection while the situation is easy to resolve; documented aerial evidence; no entrenched occupation problem.

### Case Study 5: Adjacent Land Fire — Firefighter Intelligence Support

**Scenario**: Dry-season fire breaks out on open land adjacent to AECI's south boundary; smoke visible from guard post.

**Without Drone**:
- Guard alerts Control Room; fire department called with address and basic description
- Fire department arrives with no aerial picture of the fire's size, direction, or proximity to the fence
- Tactical decisions made on partial information; resource positioning may need to be revised on arrival
- AECI management receives verbal updates; no visual record of fire progression

**With Drone**:
1. Drone deployed immediately on first smoke alert (T+3 minutes)
2. Drone reaches scene (T+8 minutes); thermal + HD feed active
3. Control Room has aerial view: fire extent, wind-driven spread direction, distance from fence
4. Fire department contacted: "Fire approx 200m x 80m, spreading north-east, approaching south fence — access via East Gate recommended"
5. Fire department arrives with a tactical picture; positions correctly on first attempt
6. Drone tracks fire boundary in real-time; alerts if spread threatens the fence line
7. Entire event documented from start to containment; footage retained for insurance claim

**Value**: Faster firefighter deployment, better tactical decisions, AECI assets protected by earlier intervention. A drone aerial briefing to the fire department is a capability no ground-level guard can provide.
