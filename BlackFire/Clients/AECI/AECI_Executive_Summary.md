## THE OPPORTUNITY

AECI Chempark currently has a physical guarding contractor managing on-site security, and BlackFire managing the perimeter fence system, alarms, and access control. However, when a fence alert triggers, the response model has a critical gap:

- **No immediate aerial assessment** — there is no on-site capability to quickly verify whether an alert is a genuine threat or a false alarm before dispatching a tactical response
- **Costly false alarm dispatches** — tactical response is sent to every alert, including the 90%+ that are false alarms caused by wind, animals, or configuration drift
- **Delayed, context-free response** — tactical responders arrive without knowing what they're responding to; no live view of the threat zone before arrival
- **No aerial evidence capture** — when a genuine breach occurs, there is no aerial documentation for SAPS

---

## THE SOLUTION

**BlackFire adds a drone-first response layer and tactical response capability directly integrated with the existing Control Room**, creating a two-tier response model for every perimeter alert:

**Tier 1 — Drone (First Responder)**: An on-site drone operator deploys the drone to the alert zone within minutes of any fence alert. The operator visually assesses the situation before any tactical dispatch decision is made.

**Tier 2 — Tactical Response Unit**: Dispatched **only when the drone confirms a genuine threat**. The tactical unit arrives with full aerial intelligence — exact breach location, scale, and visual context — already fed to them en route.

## WHY DRONE-FIRST CHANGES EVERYTHING

|----------|---|---|
| **False alarm (wind / animal)** | Tactical dispatch sent → expensive, wasted | Drone confirms false alarm → no dispatch, no cost |
| **Genuine breach** | Tactical arrives with no information | Tactical arrives briefed by live aerial feed |
| **Night-time breach** | No visibility; manual search on arrival | Thermal imaging identifies suspect before team arrives |
| **SAPS evidence** | Ground-level report only | HD aerial footage + thermal + incident timeline |
| **Multiple simultaneous alerts** | One at a time; expensive | Drone confirms priorities; team sent to confirmed threat |

## WHY THIS MODEL WORKS FOR AECI

- **Complements existing contractor** — no disruption to current physical guarding arrangement
- **Builds on existing BlackFire relationship** — same team who manages the fence now responds to its alerts
- **Cost-efficient** — 90%+ of costly false alarm dispatches eliminated
- **Legally defensible** — aerial footage provides court-admissible evidence for genuine breaches
- **Scalable** — drone programme can extend to CCTV gap coverage, building surveillance, and special operations
- **Public perimeter risk managed** — boundary sections exposed to public land are monitored aerially; informal encampments and fire risks on adjacent open areas are detected early, before they escalate
- **Fire response intelligence** — when a fire occurs on or adjacent to the site, the drone is airborne immediately, providing the fire department with live aerial imagery of the fire's extent, spread direction, and proximity to AECI infrastructure before they arrive on-scene
- **Workforce optimisation** — with drone handling perimeter patrol and surveillance coverage, the physical guard complement can be right-sized over time; the drone does not replace access control or incident response, but it removes the patrol burden that currently ties up a significant portion of the guard team
- **Asset ownership option** — the drone is stationed permanently on AECI premises; AECI has the option to own it outright, ensuring aerial capability remains an AECI asset regardless of service provider arrangements in future

## BEYOND ALERT RESPONSE: THE BROADER STRATEGIC CASE

The drone-first model is positioned in this proposal primarily as a response capability improvement. However, the same on-site drone delivers strategic value across a broader operational picture that is worth noting for leadership consideration:

| Capability | Strategic Value |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled aerial patrols** | GPS-logged proof of perimeter coverage; anomaly detection independent of guard attention |
| **Public boundary monitoring** | Identifies squatting, informal occupation, and fire risk adjacent to perimeter before ground-level detection |
| **Fire department support** | Aerial briefing to firefighters before arrival; real-time spread monitoring during an active fire |
| **Guard complement reduction** | Drone performs patrol functions; guard numbers can be optimised toward access control and response |
| **Drone ownership** | AECI can own the drone outright — aerial capability as a permanent facility asset, not a borrowed service |

A fuller treatment of these strategic benefits is captured in the companion document: **AECI-SEC-2026-SBP-001 — Drone Technology: Strategic Business Case**.
