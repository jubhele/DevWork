## Goal
Widen the Umlilo Portal vertical nav rail (left sidebar) which was 56px wide — too narrow to comfortably display the nav item labels.

## Model Recommendation
Task tier: 1-Fast
Recommended model: Haiku 4.5   Trust score: 9/10

## Decisions
- Phase 1: Increased sidebar from 56px → 80px. Later widened to 160px.
- Phase 2: Sidebar extended to top:0 (full height); logo added at top of sidebar (38px tall, max-width:136px inside 160px rail).
- In portal state: `.pub-brand` and the theme button are hidden from `#pub-nav`; `#pub-nav` shifted `left:160px` so it sits beside the sidebar without overlap.
- `#pmain` padding-left updated across all breakpoints: 192px (desktop), 176px (≤1024px), 76px (≤640px).
- On mobile (≤640px): sidebar collapses to 64px, `#pnav-logo` hidden, `#pub-nav` shifted to left:64px.

## Learnings
- The portal sidebar width and the main content padding-left are tightly coupled; both must be updated together.
- Moving the sidebar to top:0 requires hiding the logo from #pub-nav and shifting it right by the sidebar width in portal state.
- The api/ subfolder has a separate horizontal nav layout — it is independent of the vertical rail in portal.css.
