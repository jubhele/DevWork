## Decisions

- Adjusted `#pmain` padding-left from 88px → 112px (desktop), 72px → 96px (1024px breakpoint), 60px → 76px (640px breakpoint) to maintain consistent 32px breathing room between sidebar edge and content.
- Widened `.pnav-sep` from 32px → 52px with slight margin increase to fill the wider rail proportionally.
- Only portal.css (vertical rail version) was changed — api/portal.css uses a horizontal nav and was not affected.
