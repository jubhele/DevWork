My Dietary Plan
7-Day Meal Plan & Grocery Budget — Tbilisi, Georgia
Built for an 18-year-old footballer training on a weekly grocery budget of 350 GEL (~$130 / ~R2,180). The
week is split into 5 training days and 2 rest days — carbs shift up on training days to fuel performance and
recovery, while protein stays steady every day for muscle repair.

Practical tips
 Buy meat and eggs in bulk once a week at Dezerter Bazaar — cheaper per kg and fewer trips.
 Restock vegetables/greens 2–3x a week since they spoil fast.
 Dairy, rice, and pantry basics keep well from a weekly supermarket run (Nikora/Carrefour).
 Shift the fish/extra-beef buffer to the hardest training day or the day right after for recovery.

Where these shops are / what they're good for
 Dezerter Bazaar — Tbilisi's largest farmers market, near the central train station. Best for meat, cheese,
produce, nuts, and dried fruit at the lowest prices in the city.
 Nikora / Carrefour — supermarket chains found in every district. Best for dairy, packaged rice/pasta, oil,
honey, and pantry staples, plus reliable stock when the bazaar is out of something.
 Corner shops (magazias) — small neighborhood stores on almost every block. Good for bread, eggs,
and topping up basics between bigger shopping trips.
 Local bakeries — freshest shotis puri (bread), usually cheaper and better quality than supermarket bread.
