## 1. Purpose of the Agreement

The Agency is engaged to arrange and facilitate the placement of the Player with a professional football club in Georgia. The specific club shall be **named in Annex A to this Agreement before the First Payment falls due**. The Agency shall not substitute the club named in Annex A without the prior written consent of the Paying Parent.

## 3. Refund and Failure of Placement

3.1 If, by **30 September 2026**, the Player has not signed a club contract on materially the terms set out in Section 6 and been registered with the club, the Agency shall refund the First Payment in full within 14 days of written demand.

3.2 If the placement fails for reasons attributable to the Player (documented refusal to sign, failure to travel, disciplinary withdrawal), the Agency may retain documented direct costs incurred, up to a maximum of EUR 1,500, and shall refund the balance.

3.3 The Second Payment is not payable at all if the condition in clause 2.3 is not met.

## 4. FIFA and Regulatory Compliance

4.1 The Agency warrants that this Agreement and its fee structure comply with the FIFA Football Agent Regulations and the regulations of the Georgian Football Federation, **including all provisions concerning minors** if the Player is under 18 years of age at the date of signature.

4.2 If any competent authority determines that the fee, in whole or part, is impermissible, the impermissible amount shall be refunded to the Paying Parent within 14 days.

## 5. Authorization to Proceed

By signing, the Paying Parent and Representative authorize the Agency to: negotiate with the club named in Annex A; carry out registration and administrative processes; arrange accommodation; and finalize the Player's club contract on the terms in Section 6.

## 6. Club Contract — Principal Terms

The club contract arranged by the Agency shall include:

- **Duration:** Six (6) months, with an option for a further six (6) months based on performance, discipline, fitness, and contribution.
- **Monthly salary:** Not less than 500 GEL, paid monthly.
- **Accommodation:** Private apartment provided by the club at no cost to the Player or the Paying Parent.
- **Transfermarkt:** The Player shall be listed on Transfermarkt within 30 days of registration.
- **Salary review:** During any extension, the club may negotiate an increased monthly salary.
