## Bird and Aquarium-Fish Breeders

No reliable public registry of companion-bird or ornamental-fish breeders in Tbilisi was found during this research. Zootopia publicly confirms live-fish categories, while ZooCity and other shops confirm bird and fish supplies, but this does not prove that they breed the animals themselves.

Before buying a bird or fish, ask the seller to provide:

- The breeder or importer name and contact details
- Hatch or birth date where applicable
- Species and scientific name
- Captive-bred confirmation
- Health history and current diet
- Any required ring, permit, import, or veterinary documentation
- A written return or health policy

Do not accept wild-caught native birds or a seller who refuses to identify the animal's source. For an aquarium, buy and cycle the tank before purchasing fish.

## Legal and Welfare Verification for Dogs and Cats

Georgia's Law on Pet Animals now requires a breeding facility to register with the municipality. The facility must display its registration document, keep a breeding book and litter records, and identify and register litters. Existing facilities have a transition deadline of 1 January 2027 for municipal registration, while relevant database-access provisions took effect during 2026.

Before paying a breeder, verify:

1. Municipal breeding-facility registration or evidence of the current transition process.
2. Registered kennel or cattery name and pedigree organisation.
3. Breeding book and litter-registration documents.
4. Microchip or other required identification and registration details.
5. Vaccination, deworming, veterinary examination, and pet passport records.
6. Breed-specific health tests for both parents.
7. An opportunity to meet the mother and view the environment where the litter is raised.
8. A written sale agreement identifying the breeder, buyer, animal, health terms, and return conditions.
9. No handover before the animal is appropriately weaned, socialised, and cleared by a veterinarian.
10. No cash deposit to an anonymous seller without verifiable registration and a physical inspection.

Official law: <https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/6566730?impose=translateEn>
