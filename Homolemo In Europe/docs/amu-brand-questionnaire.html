Amu — Your Brand, Your Words

This isn't a test. Answer honestly, in your own words — voice notes are fine. Your answers decide how the world gets introduced to you. Short answers are fine; real answers matter more than clever ones.

Who you are

1. How do you want people to say your name — Homolemo, Amu, or Shange? And which do you want on the back of your shirt?

2. In one sentence, who are you when you're on the ball?

Example answers: "I slow the game down." / "I make the pass nobody saw."

3. You've been compared to Doctor Khumalo, Jomo Sono, Teko Modise, Messi, and Zidane. Which comparison feels most true — and which one do you not agree with?

4. What's the one moment on a football pitch you're proudest of so far?

Your story

5. Why Europe? Answer like you're telling a friend, not a scout.

6. What are you most nervous about in Georgia? (This stays private unless you choose otherwise — but knowing it helps us tell your story honestly.)

7. Who, besides family, believed in you first?

8. Finish this: "In five years, I want people to say Shange is ______."

The content (what you'll actually enjoy)

9. Which would you actually enjoy filming? Rank them: training clips / skill challenges / day-in-the-life vlogs / talking to camera / match highlights only.

10. Is there anything you do NOT want shown online? (School, friends, religion, family members — your line is the line.)

11. Which footballers' social media do you rate? What do they do that you like?

12. Music: what are you listening to before matches? (This becomes the soundtrack of your clips.)

The deal between us

13. Dad handles the business so you handle the football. What decisions do you want to always be asked about before we post or sign anything?

14. What should happen if you ever feel the brand stuff is getting in the way of your football? Say it now so we have the rule before we need it.
